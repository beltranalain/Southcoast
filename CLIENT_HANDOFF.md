## What you now own

Your own live-streaming platform — a branded website where you go live, keep your
audience, and get paid directly. No more per-seat streaming subscriptions, and no
more giving away 30% of your tips.

**You can:**
- **Go live from your browser** — no OBS, no downloads. Just pick your camera and mic and hit *Go Live*.
- **Stream to your site and YouTube at the same time.** Keep your YouTube audience while building your own home base.
- **Bring on guests** — send a link, they join from their browser (video, audio, or both). You admit them to the show when you're ready.
- **Share your screen** — show a video, slides, or another tab, with full-screen, picture-in-picture, or side-by-side layouts.
- **Put graphics on the broadcast** — lower-third banners and pinned comments you can drag anywhere on screen.
- **Run a live chat** — viewers sign in (Google or email) to comment, so it's real people with real names, not spam. You can **timeout, ban, or remove** anyone.
- **Take tips** — viewers tip you during the show; it pops on screen and in chat. The money lands in **your** account, minus only the standard card fee (~3%) — not 30%.
- **Manage everything** from one dashboard — your logo and colors, shows, schedule, videos, viewers, and tips.

## Getting started (your Studio)

1. **Sign in** at `yoursite.com/admin` with the login we set up for you.
2. **Go Live** → pick camera + mic → *Go Live*. You're now on your site and YouTube.
3. **Guests** tab → copy the invite link, send it, and **Admit** them when they join.
4. **Sources** → *Share screen* to show a video or tab; toggle Full / PIP / Split.
5. **On air** → type a banner or pin a comment; drag it wherever you want on the video.
6. **Chat** → moderate viewers (timeout / ban) right from the studio.
7. **Branding / Content / Schedule** → update your look, shows, and upcoming times anytime.

Your viewers see it all on your public site — the live player, the chat, and the tip button.

## Your accounts (what powers it)

Your platform runs on your **own** accounts, so you own your data and audience. These are yours:

| Service | What it does | Typical cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Vercel** | Hosts your website | Free — or ~$20/mo if you grow |
| **Firebase** | Sign-ins, viewer accounts, your content | Free at your scale |
| **Cloudflare** | Live video, recordings, chat, guests | ~$5/mo + usage |
| **YouTube API** | Pulls in your videos + live status | Free |
| **Stripe** | Your tips / payments | No monthly fee; ~3% per tip |
| **Resend** *(optional)* | Contact-form emails | Free tier |
| **Your domain** | e.g. southcoastcane.com | ~$10–15/year |

**Realistic total: about $5–30/month plus streaming usage** — a fraction of what stacked subscriptions cost, and it scales with how much you actually use.
