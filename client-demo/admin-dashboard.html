First broadcast, step by step

From cold start to on air in a few minutes.
  1. Set the show and destinationsSchedule → pick the show → choose Site + YouTube + Facebook. Routing is remembered per show.
  2. Open Go LiveAllow camera + mic. Add guests to the green room and admit them when ready.
  3. Add your graphicsLower-third for the topic, a branded scene, pin the best comment.
  4. Push liveOne button streams to every destination at once. The merged chat lights up.
  5. End & saveEnd broadcast — the show saves to Videos as a VOD automatically.

Roles — who can see what

Set roles under Team. A limited role literally cannot open the pages it's not allowed to.

Owner / Manager

Full Studio. Manager can't touch Team.

Host

Runs and schedules shows.

Moderator

Chat + user moderation during live.

Viewer

No Studio access — public site only.

Quick troubleshooting

The usual first checks.
YouTube not showing the stream?
Check the channel is connected under Content and the key hasn't rotated.
Chat empty?
Firebase Realtime must be connected — see Settings → Integrations.
Cost warning?
Only on-site viewers bill. Push more traffic to YouTube to lower cost.
